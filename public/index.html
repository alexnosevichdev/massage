About Me

I'm Anna, a certified massage therapist with over 12 years of hands-on experience. My practice blends classical European techniques with deep-tissue therapy, creating personalized sessions that target your specific needs.

Whether you're dealing with chronic pain, recovering from an injury, or simply need a restorative escape from daily stress, I provide a safe, comfortable environment where healing begins.

I hold certifications in Swedish Massage, Sports Massage, and Myofascial Release. Every session is tailored — because no two bodies are alike.
